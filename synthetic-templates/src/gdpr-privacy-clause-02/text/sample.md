# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, CloudVault Systems B.V. (the "Processor") shall process personal data on behalf of Meridian Health Analytics Ltd. (the "Controller") solely in accordance with the Controller's documented instructions and applicable data protection law. The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours, upon becoming aware of any personal data breach affecting the processed data. Personal data shall be retained for no longer than 36 months following termination of the underlying service agreement, after which it shall be securely deleted or anonymized, and all such processing shall take place entirely within the European Economic Area with no onward transfer outside the EEA. The Controller shall pay the Processor a monthly data protection compliance fee of 1250.00 for maintaining these safeguards, and the Processor shall respond to any verified data subject access, rectification, or erasure request within 14 days of receipt.
