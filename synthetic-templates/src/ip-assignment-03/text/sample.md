# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, Marcus Ellery Whitfield (the "Assignor") hereby irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to Northbridge Digital Studios, Inc. (the "Assignee") all right, title, and interest, including all worldwide intellectual property rights, in and to the character designs, storyboards, and source code for the mobile application known as 'Lumen Trails', in consideration of a payment of $18500.00, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged. The Assignor waives any moral rights he may hold in the assigned works to the fullest extent permitted by law. The Assignor agrees to reasonably cooperate with the Assignee, including executing further documents and assisting in any registration proceedings, as may be necessary to perfect the Assignee's ownership of the assigned intellectual property. This Assignment shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware.
