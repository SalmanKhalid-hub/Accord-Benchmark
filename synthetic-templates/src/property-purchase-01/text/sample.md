# Clause

Greenfield Holdings LLC ('Seller') agrees to sell and Marcus T. Reilly ('Buyer') agrees to purchase the real property located at 482 Willow Creek Lane, Asheville, NC 28801, for a total purchase price of $425000.00. Buyer shall deposit $21250.00 in earnest money with the designated escrow agent within three business days of execution of this agreement. Buyer shall have 10 days from the effective date to complete all inspections of the property and notify Seller in writing of any objections. Since this purchase is not subject to a financing contingency, Buyer's obligation to close shall not be conditioned upon obtaining a mortgage loan or other financing. Closing shall occur on or before 2024-09-15, at which time Seller shall deliver a duly executed deed conveying marketable title to Buyer.
