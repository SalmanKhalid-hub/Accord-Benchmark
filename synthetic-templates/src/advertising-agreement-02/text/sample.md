# Clause

Northbridge Retail Group ("Advertiser") hereby engages Vantage Media Partners LLC ("Agency") to plan, purchase, and manage all media placements for the advertising campaign known as the Summer Bright Launch, commencing on 2024-06-01 and continuing through 2024-08-31. Advertiser shall pay Agency a total media budget of 125000.00 for the placement and management of the campaign across agreed channels, and Agency shall guarantee delivery of no fewer than 5000000 total impressions during the campaign term. In consideration of this engagement, Advertiser shall not engage any other agency or media buying service for competing retail-sector campaigns during the term, granting Agency sole and exclusive rights to represent Advertiser within that category. Any shortfall in guaranteed impressions shall entitle Advertiser to a pro-rata credit against future media spend, applied at Agency's next billing cycle.
