# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, Vertex Cloud Services GmbH shall process personal data on behalf of Northbridge Analytics Ltd. solely in accordance with the documented instructions of the latter and applicable data protection law, including Regulation (EU) 2016/679. In the event of any personal data breach, the Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, providing all information reasonably required to enable the Controller to comply with its own notification obligations. Personal data processed under this engagement shall be retained for no longer than 365 days following termination of the underlying services, after which it shall be securely deleted or anonymized unless a longer retention period is required by law. The Processor confirms that personal data will not be transferred outside the European Economic Area without the Controller's prior written authorization and appropriate safeguards. Any audit conducted by the Controller to verify compliance with this clause shall be subject to a service fee equal to 2.5% of the annual processing charges, and all inquiries regarding data protection matters should be directed to the Controller's Data Protection Officer at dpo@northbridgeanalytics.eu.
