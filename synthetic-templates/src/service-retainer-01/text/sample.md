# Clause

Effective as of 2024-04-01, Harborview Realty Group LLC ("Client") shall engage Calloway & Finch Advisory Partners ("Advisor") on a retainer basis, paying a monthly retainer fee of 7500.00, which shall entitle Client to up to 20 hours of advisory services per month. Any hours utilized in excess of the included monthly allotment shall be billed at a rate of 225.00 per hour, invoiced in arrears. This engagement shall automatically renew for successive monthly terms unless either party provides written notice of termination, and either party may terminate this arrangement by providing no less than 30 days prior written notice to the other party. All retainer fees paid are non-refundable and shall be applied solely to services rendered during the corresponding month.
