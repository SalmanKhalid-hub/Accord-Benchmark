# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, Northbridge Data Systems, Inc. (the "Processor") shall process personal data solely on behalf of and under the documented instructions of Larkspur Retail Group, LLC (the "Controller"). The Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, upon becoming aware of any personal data breach affecting the data processed under this Agreement. Personal data shall be retained for no longer than 90 days following termination of the underlying services, after which it shall be securely deleted or returned. The parties acknowledge that personal data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area, provided that appropriate safeguards, including standard contractual clauses, are implemented; where the Processor engages a sub-processor, it shall remain liable for a service fee adjustment of 2.5 percent to cover compliance oversight costs, and the Controller retains the right to conduct an on-site audit once per year to verify compliance with this Agreement.
