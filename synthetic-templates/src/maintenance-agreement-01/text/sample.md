# Clause

This Maintenance Agreement is entered into as of 2024-03-01 between Meridian Technical Services LLC ("Provider") and Harbor Point Manufacturing Inc. ("Client"). In consideration of an annual fee of 18500.00, Provider shall provide preventive and corrective maintenance services and shall respond to any covered service request within responseTimeHours of 4 hours from notification by Client. Provider's obligations under this Agreement are limited to a maximum of 24 service calls per year, with any additional calls billed separately at Provider's then-current hourly rates. Unless either party provides written notice of termination at least sixty days prior to the end of the then-current term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive terms of 12 months on the same terms and conditions set forth herein.
