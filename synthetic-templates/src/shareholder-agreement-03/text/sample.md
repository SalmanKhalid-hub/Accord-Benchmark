# Clause

Should Marcus Feldman wish to transfer any or all of his 5000 shares in Brightline Analytics, Inc. to a third party, he shall first give the remaining shareholders written notice of the proposed transfer at least 30 days prior to the intended transfer date, specifying the number of shares, the identity of the proposed transferee, and the offered price of $12.75 per share. The remaining shareholders, who collectively hold 62.5% of the outstanding voting shares, shall have a preemptive right to purchase such shares on the same terms before any transfer to an outside party may proceed. This right of first refusal shall become effective as of 2024-03-01 and shall remain binding on all parties for so long as they hold shares in the Company. If the remaining shareholders decline to exercise this right within the notice period, Marcus Feldman shall be free to complete the transfer to the proposed transferee on the disclosed terms.
