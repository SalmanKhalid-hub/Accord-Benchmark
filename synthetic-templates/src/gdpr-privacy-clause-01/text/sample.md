# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, Solera Cloud Services B.V. shall process personal data on behalf of Northbridge Analytics Ltd. solely in accordance with the documented instructions of the latter and in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation. The Processor shall not engage any sub-processor to carry out processing activities without the prior written authorization of the Controller, as such engagement of further sub-processing is not permitted under this clause. In the event of a personal data breach, the Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, enabling timely reporting to the Dutch Data Protection Authority where required. Personal data shall be retained for no longer than 365 days following the termination of the underlying service agreement, after which it shall be securely deleted or anonymized. The Controller shall be entitled to conduct, or appoint a third party to conduct, an annual compliance audit of the Processor's data protection practices, the cost of which shall not exceed 4500.00 per audit cycle.
