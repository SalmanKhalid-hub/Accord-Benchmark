# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, DataVault Solutions Inc. (the "Processor") shall process personal data on behalf of Meridian Health Group LLC (the "Controller") solely in accordance with the Controller's documented instructions and applicable data protection law. The Processor shall notify the Controller of any personal data breach without undue delay and in no event later than 72 hours after becoming aware of such breach. Upon termination or expiry of this Agreement, the Processor shall securely delete or return all personal data within 90 days, retaining no copies except as required by law. The Processor shall not engage any subprocessor to carry out processing activities without the Controller's prior written consent, and the Controller shall have the right to conduct an annual compliance audit, the reasonable costs of which shall not exceed 15000.00 per audit cycle.
