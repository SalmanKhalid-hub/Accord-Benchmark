# Clause

Any dispute, controversy, or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including its formation, validity, interpretation, breach, or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre in accordance with the SIAC Arbitration Rules. The arbitration shall be conducted in English, with the seat of arbitration in Singapore, before a tribunal of 3 arbitrators, and the Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Singapore. Given the parties' mutual desire for a swift resolution, the arbitral tribunal shall apply the expedited procedure available under the applicable rules regardless of the amount in dispute. Each party's liability for the counterparty's recoverable arbitration costs, including tribunal fees and reasonable legal expenses, shall not exceed 250000.00 in the aggregate. Any notice of intent to commence arbitration must be delivered to the other party no later than 2024-03-01, failing which the claim shall be deemed waived.
