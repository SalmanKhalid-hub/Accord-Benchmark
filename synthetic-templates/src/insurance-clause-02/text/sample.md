# Clause

Meridian Assurance Group shall provide commercial general liability coverage to Bramwell Logistics Inc. under Policy Number MAG-CGL-88214, with an aggregate coverage limit of 2500000.00 and a per-claim deductible of 10000.00 payable by the Policyholder before any indemnification obligation arises. Coverage under this policy shall commence on 2024-03-01 and continue in force through 2025-03-01, subject to timely payment of premiums. Upon expiration, this policy shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least thirty days prior to the expiration date. All claims must be reported to the Insurer in writing within fifteen days of the occurrence giving rise to the claim to preserve coverage rights hereunder.
