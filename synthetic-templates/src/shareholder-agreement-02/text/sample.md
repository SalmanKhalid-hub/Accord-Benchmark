# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, should Marguerite Voss wish to transfer any portion of her 18.5% shareholding in Halcyon Robotics Ltd. to a third party, she shall first offer such shares to the remaining shareholders at a price of $425000.00, reflecting the bona fide terms of the proposed transfer. The remaining shareholders shall have 30 days from receipt of written notice of the proposed transfer within which to elect to purchase all, but not less than all, of the offered shares on the same terms. Any transfer completed pursuant to this right of first refusal requires prior approval of the board of directors before it shall become effective. If no shareholder exercises this right within the notice period, Marguerite Voss may proceed to transfer the shares to the identified third party on terms no more favorable than those offered to the other shareholders.
