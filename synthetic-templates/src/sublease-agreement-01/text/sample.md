# Clause

Marcus Feldstein, as Sublessor, hereby subleases to Bright Horizon Consulting LLC, as Sublessee, the premises located at 745 Kingsley Avenue, Suite 210, Chicago, Illinois 60614, for a term commencing on 2024-06-01 and expiring on 2025-05-31. Sublessee shall pay Sublessor monthly rent in the amount of 2350.00, due on the first day of each month, together with a security deposit of 2350.00 payable upon execution of this sublease. The parties acknowledge that written consent from the prime landlord has been duly obtained prior to the execution of this sublease, satisfying any consent requirement contained in the master lease. This sublease is subject to and subordinate to all terms and conditions of the master lease between Sublessor and the prime landlord, and Sublessee agrees to comply with all such terms as if it were the original tenant thereunder.
