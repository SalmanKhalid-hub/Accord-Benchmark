# Clause

Meridian Technical Services LLC shall provide preventative and corrective maintenance services to Harborview Medical Center commencing on 2024-03-01, in exchange for an annual fee of $48500.00 payable in accordance with the invoicing schedule set forth herein. The Service Provider agrees to respond to any covered service request within 4 hours of notification, and shall perform up to a maximum of 24 service calls per year at no additional charge beyond the annual fee. This Agreement shall terminate automatically at the end of the initial term and will not renew unless the parties execute a new written agreement prior to expiration. Any payment not received within thirty (30) days of the invoice date shall accrue a late payment penalty at a rate of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance until paid in full.
