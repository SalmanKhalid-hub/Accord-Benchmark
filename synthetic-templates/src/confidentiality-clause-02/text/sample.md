# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, Vantage Consulting Group LLC (the "Receiving Party") agrees to hold in strict confidence all proprietary and non-public information disclosed to it by Northbridge Analytics Inc. (the "Disclosing Party") in connection with their business relationship, and shall not disclose such information to any third party for a period of 5 years from the date of disclosure. Upon termination or expiration of this arrangement, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all confidential materials, including copies and derivatives thereof, within 15 days of the Disclosing Party's written request. The parties acknowledge that any unauthorized use or disclosure of confidential information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone would be inadequate, and accordingly agree that the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity. In the event of a breach of this clause, the Receiving Party shall be liable for liquidated damages in the amount of 50000.00, without prejudice to any further claims for actual damages. This clause shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware.
