# Clause

In consideration of Meridian Commercial Bank extending credit facilities to Harrington Logistics LLC, Michael J. Harrington irrevocably and unconditionally guarantees the due and punctual payment of all sums owed by Harrington Logistics LLC, up to a maximum aggregate liability of 250000.00 USD, arising on or after 2024-03-01. This guarantee shall remain in full force and effect until 2027-03-01, unless earlier released in writing by Meridian Commercial Bank. The guarantor's obligations hereunder are joint and several with those of the principal debtor and any other guarantors, such that the creditor may pursue Michael J. Harrington directly for the full amount owed without first exhausting remedies against Harrington Logistics LLC. This guarantee shall survive any amendment, renewal, or extension of the underlying credit arrangement between the parties.
