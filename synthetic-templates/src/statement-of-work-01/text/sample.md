# Clause

This Statement of Work is entered into between Blackfin Consulting LLC ("Contractor") and Northgate Retail Group Inc. ("Client") for the design and implementation of a cloud-based inventory management platform. Contractor shall commence work on 2024-04-01 and shall achieve final completion no later than 2024-09-30, with progress measured against 5 discrete milestones set forth in Exhibit A. In consideration of the services rendered, Client shall pay Contractor a total fee of 185000.00, payable on a fixed-price basis rather than on a time-and-materials basis, with amounts becoming due upon Client's written acceptance of each completed milestone. All deliverables produced under this engagement shall be delivered in accordance with the specifications and acceptance criteria described in the attached exhibits.
