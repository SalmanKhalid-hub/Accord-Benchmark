# Clause

In consideration of Northbridge Commercial Bank extending credit facilities to Cavanaugh Freight Solutions LLC, the undersigned, Martin J. Cavanaugh, personally and unconditionally guarantees the due and punctual payment of all present and future indebtedness owed by Cavanaugh Freight Solutions LLC to Northbridge Commercial Bank, up to a maximum aggregate liability of 250000.00 USD. This guarantee shall take effect on 2024-03-01 and shall remain a continuing obligation binding upon the guarantor until all such indebtedness, together with accrued interest at a rate of 6.5% per annum, has been fully and finally discharged. The guarantor further agrees that Northbridge Commercial Bank may enforce this guarantee directly against Martin J. Cavanaugh without first exhausting remedies against Cavanaugh Freight Solutions LLC.
