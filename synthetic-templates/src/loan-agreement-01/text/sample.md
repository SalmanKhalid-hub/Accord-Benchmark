# Clause

Meridian Capital Partners LLC (the "Lender") agrees to extend to Vantage Robotics Inc. (the "Borrower") a loan in the principal amount of 250000.00, bearing interest at a fixed annual rate of 6.75 percent, with all outstanding principal and accrued interest due and payable in full on 2027-09-01. Should any scheduled payment remain unpaid after a grace period of 10 days following its due date, the Borrower shall pay a late payment fee of 150.00 in addition to the amount then owing. The Borrower may prepay all or any portion of the outstanding principal balance at any time without penalty, provided that any accrued interest through the date of prepayment is paid concurrently. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws applicable to the Lender's principal place of business.
