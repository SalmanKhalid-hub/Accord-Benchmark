# Clause

The Insured shall maintain a commercial general liability policy issued by Meridian Assurance Group Inc. under Policy Number MAG-2024-778145, providing coverage in an amount not less than 2500000.00 for each occurrence, subject to a deductible of 10000.00 payable by the Insured before any indemnification obligation arises. Coverage under this policy shall be effective as of 2024-03-01 and shall remain in force through 2025-03-01, with an annual premium of 48750.00 payable in accordance with the insurer's billing schedule. The parties acknowledge that the policy will renew automatically upon expiration unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least thirty days prior to the expiration date. The Insured shall furnish the Counterparty with a certificate of insurance evidencing such coverage upon request and shall promptly notify the Counterparty of any material change, cancellation, or lapse in coverage.
