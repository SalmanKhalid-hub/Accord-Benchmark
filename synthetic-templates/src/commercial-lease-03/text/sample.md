# Clause

Commencing on 2024-03-01, Brightside Retail Partners Inc. ("Tenant") shall pay Meridian Commercial Holdings LLC ("Landlord") monthly rent of 8500.00 in advance on the first day of each calendar month for the demised premises. Any payment not received within five business days of its due date shall incur a late fee equal to 5.0 percent of the overdue amount. Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of 17000.00 as security for the faithful performance of its obligations hereunder, to be held and returned in accordance with applicable law. Tenant shall have the option to renew this lease for one additional term of 5 years upon written notice delivered to Landlord no later than ninety days prior to expiration of the then-current term, it being understood that the lease shall not renew automatically absent written notice from the Tenant.
