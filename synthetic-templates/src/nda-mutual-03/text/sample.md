# Clause

This Agreement is entered into as of 2024-03-01 by and between Alderbrook Systems Inc. and Northgate Analytics LLC, each a disclosing and receiving party as context requires. Each party agrees to hold in confidence all Confidential Information disclosed by the other for a period of 36 months following the date of disclosure, and to use such information solely for the purpose of evaluating a potential business relationship. Upon written request, each receiving party shall return or destroy all tangible embodiments of the other party's Confidential Information within 15 days. The parties acknowledge that any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate, and accordingly agree that the non-breaching party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other available remedies. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware.
