# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, Supplier hereby appoints Meridian Trading Co., Ltd. as its exclusive distributor for the marketing, sale, and distribution of the Products within the Benelux region. In consideration of this appointment, Distributor shall pay Supplier a royalty of 8.5% on net sales of the Products and shall purchase from Supplier no less than 250000.00 in Products annually to maintain its distribution rights. This grant of exclusivity is subject to Distributor's continued compliance with the minimum purchase requirements set forth herein. Upon expiration of the initial term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of 2 years unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety days prior to the end of the then-current term.
