# Clause

Any dispute, controversy or claim arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including its formation, interpretation, breach or termination, shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Singapore International Arbitration Centre in accordance with the SIAC Arbitration Rules in force at the time the notice of arbitration is submitted. The arbitration shall be seated in Singapore, conducted in English, and heard before a tribunal of 3 arbitrators appointed pursuant to the said rules. This Agreement, and any dispute or claim arising out of it, shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales, without regard to conflict of laws principles. A party wishing to commence arbitration must file its notice of arbitration within 30 days of the dispute arising, and the parties may avail themselves of the expedited procedure available under the applicable rules. This clause shall take effect as of 2024-03-01 and shall survive termination of this Agreement.
