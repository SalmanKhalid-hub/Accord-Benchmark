# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, Nimbus Cloud Solutions Inc., acting as data processor, shall notify Harrow Retail Group Ltd., the data controller, without undue delay and in no event later than 72 hours after becoming aware of any confirmed unauthorized access, disclosure, or loss of personal data. Where the incident affects 500 or more individual data subject records, the data processor's notification obligations shall extend to informing the relevant supervisory authority in accordance with applicable law. Failure to provide timely notification within the stipulated period shall entitle the data controller to liquidated damages of 10000.00, payable within thirty days of written demand. Each party shall reasonably cooperate in investigating and remediating the breach, including preserving relevant logs and forensic evidence.
