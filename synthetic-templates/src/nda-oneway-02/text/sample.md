# Clause

This Agreement is entered into as of 2024-03-01 by and between Meridian Biotech Inc., as the Disclosing Party, and Calder Analytics LLC, as the Receiving Party, in connection with the Receiving Party's evaluation of certain confidential information disclosed by Meridian Biotech Inc. The Receiving Party agrees to hold all such Confidential Information in strict confidence for a period of 5 years from the date of disclosure and shall not use or disclose it for any purpose other than the evaluation contemplated herein. Upon written request by the Disclosing Party, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information and copies thereof within 10 days of such request. The parties acknowledge that any unauthorized use or disclosure of Confidential Information may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate, and accordingly the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.
