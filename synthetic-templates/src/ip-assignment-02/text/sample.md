# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, Marcus T. Ellison (the "Assignor") hereby irrevocably assigns to Vantage Robotics Inc. (the "Assignee") all right, title, and interest in and to an autonomous drone stabilization algorithm and associated firmware, including all patents, copyrights, trade secrets, and other intellectual property rights therein throughout the world. In consideration of this assignment, the Assignee shall pay the Assignor a one-time sum of 45000.00, receipt of which shall be acknowledged upon payment. The Assignor agrees to execute all further documents and provide reasonable cooperation necessary for the Assignee to register, perfect, or enforce the assigned rights. This clause and any dispute arising therefrom shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware.
