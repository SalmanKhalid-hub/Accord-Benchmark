# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, DataVault Processing Services LLC shall notify Meridian Retail Group Inc. of any confirmed unauthorized access to, or disclosure of, personal data no later than 72 hours after becoming aware of such incident. Notification shall be delivered by written notice via email and certified mail and shall include the nature of the breach, the categories of data involved, and remedial steps taken. Where the incident affects 500 or more individual records, DataVault Processing Services LLC shall additionally notify the applicable supervisory authority in accordance with statutory requirements. The aggregate liability of DataVault Processing Services LLC arising from any failure to provide timely notification under this clause shall not exceed 250000.00 in total damages.
