# Clause

Solstice Beverages Inc. (the "Advertiser") hereby engages Meridian Creative Group LLC (the "Agency") to plan, place, and manage advertising media on its behalf for the campaign period commencing 2024-06-01 and continuing through 2024-12-31. The Advertiser shall provide a monthly media budget of $45000.00, from which the Agency shall be entitled to retain a commission of 15.0 percent as full compensation for its placement and negotiation services. The Agency shall use commercially reasonable efforts to secure no fewer than 2500000 total impressions across the agreed media channels during the campaign term. The Advertiser acknowledges that this engagement is non-exclusive, and the Agency may accept and service other clients, including competitors, during the term of this arrangement.
