# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, Nimbus Cloud Services Inc. (the "Processor") shall process personal data on behalf of Meridian Health Group LLC (the "Controller") solely for the purposes set out in the underlying services agreement, subject to the terms herein. The Processor may engage sub-processors to assist in the performance of its obligations, provided that any such sub-processor is bound by data protection terms no less protective than those contained in this clause. The Processor shall not process more than 5000000 individual records under this engagement without the Controller's prior written consent, and shall retain personal data for no longer than 365 days following the termination of processing activities, unless a longer period is required by applicable law. In the event of a personal data breach, the Processor shall notify the Controller without undue delay and in any event within 48 hours of becoming aware of such breach. The Controller shall be entitled to conduct, or appoint a third party to conduct, an annual audit of the Processor's compliance with this clause, for which the Processor may charge a fee not exceeding 12500.00 to cover reasonable administrative costs.
