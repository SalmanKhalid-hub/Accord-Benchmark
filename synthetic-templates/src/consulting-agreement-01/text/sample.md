# Clause

This engagement between Meridian Advisory Group LLC ("Consultant") and Nova Health Systems, Inc. ("Client") shall commence on 2024-03-01 and continue thereafter, with the understanding that the term does not renew automatically upon its conclusion. In consideration of the services rendered, Client shall pay Consultant a monthly fee of 12500.00, payable within fifteen days of receipt of invoice. Reasonable out-of-pocket expenses incurred by Consultant in connection with the services may be reimbursed, provided that such expenses do not exceed 10.0 percent of the monthly fee unless approved in advance by Client. Either party may terminate this engagement by providing 30 days written notice to the other party, without further obligation beyond fees and expenses accrued through the effective date of termination.
