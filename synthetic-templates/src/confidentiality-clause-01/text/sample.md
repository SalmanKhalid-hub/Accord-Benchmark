# Clause

Effective as of 2024-03-01, Northbridge Consulting LLC (the "Receiving Party") agrees to hold in strict confidence all proprietary and non-public information disclosed to it by Meridian Biotech Inc. (the "Disclosing Party") in connection with their business relationship. The Receiving Party's confidentiality obligations under this clause shall remain in force for a period of 5 years following the Effective Date, regardless of any earlier termination of the underlying agreement. Upon written request, the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all confidential materials within 15 days and certify such destruction in writing. The parties acknowledge that any unauthorized disclosure or use of confidential information would cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages alone may be inadequate, and accordingly the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief in addition to liquidated damages of 250000.00 for any material breach. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware.
